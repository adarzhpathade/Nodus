# NODUS — Backend Test Cases (v1)

> **Owner: BE.** Exhaustive backend test suite specifications for auth, permissions, curriculum integrity, progress engine, RAG retrieval, quizzes, offline assignments, and notifications.
> Task: `BE-11.2`. Note for FE: FE merges these test cases into [`docs/test_cases.md`](test_cases.md).

---

## 1. Overview & Verification Strategy

This document provides 50 rigorous backend test cases covering business rules, security boundaries, mathematical formulas, and AI flows:
- **Authentication & Roles:** Session checks, role enforcement, and onboarding gatekeeping.
- **Locked Semesters:** Strict rejection of future academic semesters (`LOCKED` / 423).
- **Notes Privacy:** Absolute owner isolation; zero leakage across students or to faculty.
- **Faculty Scoping:** Subject-section teaching matrix permissions.
- **Resource Section Visibility:** Cohort-level access filtering on faculty lecture materials.
- **Progress Calculation Engine:** Worked mathematical examples matching [`docs/progress_logic.md`](progress_logic.md).
- **AI Tutor & RAG Retrieval:** Cosine distance thresholds, citation formatting, and syllabus fallback.
- **Quizzes & Mastery:** Generation, source hash caching, and topic coverage blocking.
- **Offline Assignments & Marking:** Paper submission workflow, score boundary validation, and tracker metrics.
- **Notifications & Cron Reminders:** Event triggers and automated deadline reminders.
